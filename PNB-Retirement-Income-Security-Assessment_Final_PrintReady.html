Before you begin

Welcome to your Retirement Income & Security Check

This self-assessment is designed to help you understand:

  • Whether your current income can support your desired retirement lifestyle.
  • How rising costs may affect your future expenses.
  • Whether your spouse may have sufficient income to maintain their lifestyle if you are not around.
  • Whether your retirement savings appear aligned with your future financial needs.

A few things to keep in mind

  • You may use approximate figures if exact values are not readily available.
  • The assessment takes only a few minutes to complete.
  • Your responses remain only on this device and are not stored or shared.
  • The results are indicative and intended to support retirement planning discussions.
